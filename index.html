Change of Subject公式的主項變換

What is the subject?甚麼是主項？

The subject is the letter standing alone on the left. Changing the subject means making another letter stand alone.主項是單獨在左邊的字母。主項變換就是使另一個字母單獨留下。

v = u + at
Subject is v主項是 v
a = v − ut
Same formula, subject is now a同一條公式，主項變為 a

Keep the balance保持平衡

Do the same to both sides to keep it balanced. Green box = x, each yellow block = 1.兩邊做相同的運算，才能保持平衡。綠色盒子 = x，每個黃色方塊 = 1。

x + 3 = 7

Undo in reverse order逆運算，次序倒轉

Undo the operations in reverse order, last one first.用逆運算，由最後一步開始倒轉還原。

Rules to remember記住這些規則

Operation運算Undo with逆運算
+ a− a
− a+ a
× a÷ a
÷ a× a
  1. Do the same operation to both sides.兩邊做相同的運算。
  2. Undo the operations in reverse order: usually + and − first, then × and ÷.以倒轉的次序還原：通常先處理 + 和 −，再處理 × 和 ÷。
  3. Write the new subject alone on the left.把新的主項單獨寫在左邊。

Shortcut (移項變號): when a term moves across the = sign, + becomes − and × becomes ÷.移項變號：一項越過等號時，+ 變 −，× 變 ÷。

Common mistakes常見錯誤

x = y − 5 ÷ 3x = (y − 5) ÷ 3Divide the whole side, not just one term.要除整邊，不是只除一項。
y = 5 − 2x ⇒ x = (y − 5) ÷ 2Keep the sign: divide by −2.保留負號：要除以 −2。
