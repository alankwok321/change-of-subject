Change of Subject公式的主項變換

When the subject appears more than once: collect the like terms, then group by taking out the common factor.主項出現多於一次時：先集中同類項，再抽出公因式。

When the subject appears more than once主項出現多於一次

If the subject is in only one term, undo the operations. If it is in two or more terms, undoing alone will never leave it by itself.如果主項只在一項之中，用逆運算還原便可。如果主項在兩項或以上，單靠還原不能使它單獨留下。

Spot the like terms找出同類項

Terms that contain the subject are like terms. They must be collected on the same side.含有主項的項是同類項，要集中在同一邊。

Group: take out the common factor抽出公因式

Once the like terms are together, take the subject out as a common factor. Now it appears only once.同類項集中後，把主項當作公因式抽出，它便只出現一次。

The method步驟

  1. Clear fractions and expand brackets.清除分數，展開括號。
  2. Collect the subject terms on one side, all other terms on the other.把主項的同類項集中在一邊，其他項放在另一邊。
  3. Group: take out the subject as a common factor.抽出公因式：把主項抽出來。
  4. Divide both sides by the whole bracket.兩邊除以整個括號。

移項變號: when a term crosses the = sign, + becomes − and − becomes +.移項變號：一項越過等號時，+ 變 −，− 變 +。
